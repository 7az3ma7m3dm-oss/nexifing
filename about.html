NEXIFING is a development studio based in Egypt, founded by Hazem and Eyad.

01 — STORY

Why we started

NEXIFING started because Eyad got tired of watching good ideas fail behind bad code. Businesses would come with a beautiful design and broken backends — or solid infrastructure and ugly frontends.

So we decided to do all of it. Design, code, deploy, maintain. One team, one standard, end to end. No handoffs. No "that's not our department."

Today we work with gaming platforms, small businesses and startups across Egypt and the wider Middle East. We take a limited number of projects per quarter, because fewer clients means more time on each.

If you're here, it's probably because you want something built properly — not fast and disposable. That's exactly what we do.

— Eyad & Hazem, Co-Founders
