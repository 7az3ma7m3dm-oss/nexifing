ABOUT US

A small studio
with big standards.

NEXIFING is a development studio based in Egypt, founded by Hazem and Eyad. We build websites, backends and infrastructure — end to end.

01 — STORY

Why we started

NEXIFING started because Eyad got tired of watching good ideas fail behind bad code. Businesses would come with a beautiful design and broken backends — or solid infrastructure and ugly frontends. They'd tried one agency for the design, another for the code, a third for the hosting, then spent months chasing everyone when something broke.

So we decided to do all of it. Design, code, deploy, maintain. One team, one standard, end to end. No handoffs. No "that's not our department." No excuses when things go wrong — because there is no other department to blame.

Today we work with gaming platforms, small businesses and startups across Egypt and the wider Middle East. We take a limited number of projects per quarter, because fewer clients means more time on each — and a finished product instead of a half-built mess.

If you're here, it's probably because you want something built properly — not fast and disposable. That's exactly what we do.

— Eyad & Hazem, Co-Founders
