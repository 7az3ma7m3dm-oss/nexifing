01 — OUR STORY

We started NEXIFING because we were tired of watching good ideas fail behind bad code.

Businesses would come to us with beautiful designs and broken backends — or solid infrastructure and ugly frontends. They'd hire one agency for the design, another for the code, a third for the hosting, and then spend months chasing everyone when something broke.

So we decided to do all of it. Design, code, deploy, maintain. One team, one standard, end to end. No handoffs, no "that's not our department", no excuses when things go wrong.

Today we work with gaming platforms, small businesses and startups across the Middle East and Europe — shipping products that actually stay online. We take a limited number of projects per quarter, so we can actually care about every one.

03 — PRINCIPLES

How we think

Six principles that shape every project we take on.

01

Ship working software

Nothing leaves our hands until it actually works. No "we'll fix that later", no placeholder buttons, no fake features.

02

Own the whole stack

Design, code, deploy, maintain. We don't hand off responsibility to other vendors — everything is on us.

03

Write for the next dev

Every line is documented, every decision is written down. If you leave us tomorrow, someone else can take over without a two-week handoff.

04

Say no when it matters

If something is a bad idea, we'll tell you. If a deadline is unrealistic, we'll say so. Better an honest no than a broken promise.

05

Security is not optional

Every server hardened, every API authenticated, every secret rotated. We treat your data like it's ours.

06

Stay small on purpose

We take limited projects per quarter. Fewer clients means more time on each — and better results for everyone.
