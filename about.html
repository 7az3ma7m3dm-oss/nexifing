NEXIFING is a development studio based in Egypt, founded by Hazem and Eyad. We build websites, backends, and infrastructure — end to end.

01 — STORY

NEXIFING started because I was tired of watching good ideas fail behind bad code.

Businesses would come to me with beautiful designs and broken backends — or solid infrastructure and ugly frontends. They'd hired one agency for the design, another for the code, a third for the hosting, and then spent months chasing everyone when something broke.

So I decided to do all of it. Design, code, deploy, maintain. One team, one standard, end to end. No handoffs. No "that's not our department." No excuses when things go wrong — because there is no other department to blame.

Today I work with gaming platforms, small businesses, and startups across Egypt and the wider Middle East. I take a limited number of projects per quarter, because fewer clients means more time on each — and a finished product instead of a half-built mess.

If you're here, it's probably because you want something built properly — not fast and disposable. That's exactly what I do.

— Eyad & Hazem, Co-Founders

03 — PRINCIPLES

How we think

Six principles that shape every project we take on. These aren't marketing — they're filters for what we say yes to.

01

Ship working software

Nothing leaves our hands until it actually works. No "we'll fix that later", no placeholder buttons, no fake features.

02

Own the whole stack

Design, code, deploy, maintain. We don't hand off responsibility to other vendors — everything is on us.

03

Write for the next dev

Every line is documented, every decision is written down. If you leave us tomorrow, someone else can take over without a two-week handoff.

04

Say no when it matters

If something is a bad idea, we'll tell you. If a deadline is unrealistic, we'll say so. Better an honest no than a broken promise.

05

Security is not optional

Every server hardened, every API authenticated, every secret rotated. We treat your data like it's ours.

06

Stay small on purpose

We take a limited number of projects per quarter. Fewer clients means more time on each one — and a finished product instead of a half-built mess.
