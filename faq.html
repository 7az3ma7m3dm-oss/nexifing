FAQ

Common questions

Everything you might want to know before starting a project.

How much does a website cost?

Starter landing pages start at 3,000 EGP (~$60 USD). Full multi-page sites with admin panels start at 8,000 EGP. Full-stack builds start at 15,000 EGP. Every project gets a fixed quote before we start.

How long does it take?

Landing pages: 3–7 days. Multi-page sites: 1–2 weeks. Full applications: 2–4 weeks. Timelines are agreed upfront and rarely slip.

Do I own the code?

Yes. 100%. You get the full source code, database access and hosting credentials on final delivery. No lock-in, no subscriptions to us.

What if something breaks after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes after launch. After that, we offer optional monthly maintenance plans starting at 1,500 EGP.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide. Pricing is in EGP or USD depending on your location. Communication is English or Arabic.

What payment methods do you accept?

Bank transfer, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash and PayPal for international clients. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.

Do you offer refunds?

The 50% deposit covers the work done up to that point and is non-refundable. If you're unhappy at any stage, we'll work with you to fix it before final delivery.

Can I see progress while you work?

Yes. You get access to a live preview URL from day one and progress updates every few days. No month-long silence.

What if I need changes after delivery?

Minor tweaks during the 30-day window are free. Larger changes or new features are quoted separately — usually smaller than the original project.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, on request. Just mention it in your initial message and we'll sort it before any details are shared.
