PRICING & PAYMENT

Money questions

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A landing page starts around 3,000 EGP. A full web application with a backend and admin panel usually starts at 15,000 EGP. We'll give you a fixed quote after the first conversation — no hourly billing, no surprises.

How do payments work?

Typically 50% up front, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we split into 3–4 milestones, each tied to a specific deliverable you can see and approve.

What payment methods do you accept?

InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer (Egyptian banks), and crypto (USDT / BTC) for international clients.

Is the first consultation free?

Yes. The first call or message thread is free. We discuss your project, tell you roughly what it costs, and you decide whether to proceed.

Are prices negotiable?

Prices are already set to be fair for the scope. If your budget is lower, we can discuss a smaller scope — but we don't discount the same work.

What if the project goes over budget?

It can't. The quote is fixed. If scope changes, we discuss the addition, quote it, and get written approval before we touch it. No surprise bills.

TIMELINE & PROCESS

How long it takes

How long does a project take?

Landing pages: 3–7 days. Full websites: 2–4 weeks. Complex apps: 4–8 weeks. We agree on a timeline up front and stick to it. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

What if I need changes during the project?

Minor changes are included. Major scope additions are quoted separately and agreed before we start them — so there are no surprise bills at the end.

Can you work on a tight deadline?

Sometimes. Tell us the deadline up front and we'll be honest about whether it's realistic. We'd rather tell you it can't be done than ship broken software.

How will we communicate during the project?

Whatever works best for you — WhatsApp, Discord, or email. You get updates at every milestone, and you can ask questions any time. No account managers, no middlemen.

OWNERSHIP & SECURITY

Who owns what

Do I own the code?

Always. Everything we build is yours — the source code, the deployment, the domain. We just help you get it live and keep it running. No lock-in, no proprietary platforms, no ransom for access.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, on request. If your project involves sensitive information, we'll sign an NDA before any details are shared.

How do you handle security?

Every server is hardened, every API is authenticated, every secret is rotated. We follow standard security practices — encrypted connections, rate limiting, and strict access control. We treat your data like it's ours.

Where is my data stored?

Depends on the project. Most of our backends run on Firebase / Firestore (Google Cloud), with additional servers on Hetzner or DigitalOcean. You own all the accounts — we just configure them.

SUPPORT & DELIVERY

After launch

What happens after the project is delivered?

Every project includes 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, we offer optional monthly retainers for updates, monitoring, and support. If you'd rather handle it yourself, we make sure the code is well documented so you can.

Do you handle hosting and domains too?

Yes. We configure hosting, connect your domain, set up SSL, and manage deployment. We can host on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or a VPS — whichever fits your project best. You own the account, we just help you set it up.

Can you take over a project another developer started?

Yes — we do this often. Send us the codebase or a link to the repo and we'll audit it, tell you what's salvageable and what needs rebuilding, and give you a plan.

Do you offer refunds?

If we fail to deliver what we agreed on, you get a full refund of what's been paid. If you cancel mid-project, you pay only for the work completed up to that point.

TECHNICAL

The technical bit

What stack do you build with?

Mostly React/Next.js on the frontend, Node.js or Firebase on the backend, PostgreSQL or Firestore for data, and deploy to Vercel/Netlify or a Hetzner/DigitalOcean VPS. We'll recommend the right tools for your project, not the trendy ones.

Do you design too, or just build?

Both. We handle logo design, UI/UX, design systems, and brand guidelines — everything from the first sketch to the final pixel. If you already have a designer, we work with their files instead.

Will the site work on mobile?

Yes — every project is mobile-first. We test on real devices, not just the browser's responsive mode. If it looks broken on a phone, it isn't finished.

Will it be SEO-friendly?

Yes. Every site we build includes proper semantic HTML, meta tags, sitemap, robots.txt, and fast load times. For deeper SEO work (content strategy, keyword research), we can quote separately.

What about performance?

We target Lighthouse scores of 90+ on every metric. Fast load times are non-negotiable — slow sites lose customers.

WORKING WITH US

Getting started

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with clients across the Middle East and worldwide. Communication is in English or Arabic, whichever you prefer.

What if my project is unusual or complex?

We like unusual. Tell us what you're thinking — even if it's not a standard website or app. If we can build it, we'll say so. If we can't, we'll point you at someone who can.

What's the minimum project size?

The smallest thing we take on is a single landing page (starting around 3,000 EGP). Below that, we're not the right fit — you'd be better off with a template builder.

How do I get started?

Send us a message using the contact form, or reach out on WhatsApp or Discord. Tell us what you're building, your timeline, and your budget range. We'll reply within 24 hours with next steps.

Still have questions?

Message us directly. We'll answer within a few hours.
