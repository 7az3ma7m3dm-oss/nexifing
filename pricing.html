PAYMENT & BILLING

How it works

How do payments work?

Typically 50% up front, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we split into 3–4 milestones, each tied to a specific deliverable.

What payment methods do you accept?

InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer (Egyptian banks), and crypto (USDT / BTC) for international clients.

Do you charge for the first call?

No. The first consultation is free. We discuss your project, tell you roughly what it costs, and you decide whether to proceed.

Are prices negotiable?

Prices are already set to be fair for the scope. If your budget is lower, we can discuss a smaller scope — but we don't discount the same work.

What if the project goes over budget?

It can't — the quote is fixed. If scope changes, we discuss the addition, quote it, and get written approval before we touch it. No surprise bills.

Do you offer refunds?

If we fail to deliver what was agreed, you get a full refund of what's been paid. If you cancel mid-project, you pay only for the work completed so far.

Do I own the code after I pay?

Yes. Full ownership on final payment — source code, deployment, domain, everything. No lock-in.
