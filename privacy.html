Privacy
Policy.

⚠ This is a template. It hasn't been reviewed by a lawyer. Before relying on it as a legally binding privacy notice, have a qualified lawyer in your jurisdiction (Egypt) review it — especially if you're subject to GDPR, CCPA, or similar regulations.

Last updated: 2025

1. Who we are

NEXIFING is a web development studio owned and operated by Eyad and Hazem, based in Egypt. This policy explains what data we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it.

2. What we collect

We only collect the information you give us or that we need to run the site. Specifically:

  • Contact form submissions: your name, email, service selected, and message content.
  • WhatsApp messages: handled by WhatsApp (Meta). We receive your phone number and messages when you contact us there.
  • Discord messages: handled by Discord Inc. We receive your username and messages when you contact us there.
  • Server logs: anonymous data about visitors — IP address, browser, page visited. Kept for security and abuse prevention.
  • Website analytics: We don't currently use third-party analytics (no Google Analytics, no Meta Pixel). If this changes, this policy will be updated.

3. What we don't collect

  • We don't collect payment card details on this site. Payment happens through external services (InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, banks, crypto) and follows their privacy policies.
  • We don't run advertising trackers, retargeting pixels, or third-party profiling scripts.
  • We don't collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with data, contact us and we'll delete it.

4. How we use your data

  • To respond to your inquiry or project brief
  • To manage an active project (communication, deliverables, payments)
  • To send project updates when you've asked us to
  • To prevent abuse and secure our website

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes.

5. Cookies and local storage

This website uses minimal browser storage:

  • Cart / favorites data (if applicable): stored locally in your browser only.
  • Contact form draft: saved locally so you don't lose what you typed if the page reloads.
  • No tracking cookies. No advertising cookies. No third-party analytics cookies.

You can clear this data at any time from your browser settings.

6. Where your data lives

  • Hosting: GitHub Pages (Microsoft / GitHub). Subject to GitHub's privacy policy.
  • Domain: registered through a third-party registrar (Namecheap or Cloudflare).
  • Contact form: does not currently store data on any server — it opens WhatsApp with your message prefilled. WhatsApp handles delivery.
  • Project files: stored on our own infrastructure (GitHub, private repos, local servers).

7. Data retention

  • Contact messages: kept until the inquiry is resolved, then kept up to 2 years for record-keeping.
  • Project files and communication: kept for the duration of the project and up to 5 years afterward, for legal and dispute purposes.
  • Server logs: kept 30–90 days, then deleted.

8. Your rights

You can contact us at any time to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Correct any inaccuracies in that data
  • Delete your data (except where we're legally required to keep it)
  • Export your data in a portable format
  • Object to how we process your data

To make a request, message us on WhatsApp or Discord. We'll respond within 30 days.

9. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data: encrypted connections (HTTPS), access-controlled systems, and minimal data collection. No system is 100% secure, but we take the basics seriously.

If we ever discover a data breach affecting your information, we'll notify you within 72 hours of becoming aware of it.

10. Third-party services

When you interact with our site, these third parties may process some of your data according to their own policies:

  • GitHub — hosts this website
  • WhatsApp — messaging
  • Discord — messaging
  • Google Fonts — serves the fonts on this site

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy at any time. The latest version is always at this URL. If a change is significant, we'll note it clearly at the top.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Message us on WhatsApp, Discord, or use the contact form.
