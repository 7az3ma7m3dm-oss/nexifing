Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2025

1. Overview

NEXIFING respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we use it. We keep this short and honest — no legal padding.

2. What we collect

When you contact us or start a project, we collect:

  • Your name and email address
  • Any project details you share in messages or forms
  • Basic technical data (browser type, screen size) for site improvements

3. What we don't collect

No payment information. No passwords for your other services. No location tracking. No cookies for advertising. No third-party trackers for marketing.

4. How we use it

Your data is used solely to communicate with you about projects, deliver agreed work, and send occasional updates about your project. We do not use it for advertising or sell it to third parties.

5. Storage

We use standard tools — email providers, GitHub, Firebase — to operate. Your data lives in those systems and is protected by their security. We do not store sensitive information beyond what you choose to share.

6. Sharing

We do not share your information with anyone outside our team, unless required by law or if you specifically ask us to bring in a third party for your project.

7. Your rights

You can request to see, edit or delete any information we hold about you at any time. Just email us and we'll handle it within a few days.

8. Cookies

This site does not use tracking cookies. Your theme preference (light or dark mode) is stored locally in your own browser — it never leaves your device.

9. Third-party links

Our site links to external services such as Discord, Instagram and WhatsApp. Once you click through, their own privacy policies apply. We have no control over those.

10. Children

Our services are intended for businesses and individuals aged 16 and above. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 16.

11. Changes

We may update this policy occasionally. The current version is always available on this page, and the "last updated" date at the top shows when.

12. Contact

Questions about privacy? Contact us via the contact page or email hello@nexifing.com.

QUESTIONS?

We're an open book.

If anything here isn't clear, ask before starting a project.
