FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Nexifing.

How fast will I get my website?+

Most sites are delivered within 24 hours of your request. Complex projects with custom integrations may take up to 3 days.

Do I own the code?+

Yes, 100%. Every line of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React code that Nexifing generates for you is yours to keep, modify, and host wherever you want.

Can I request changes after delivery?+

Absolutely. Every plan includes unlimited revision requests for 7 days after delivery.

What tech stack do you use?+

We generate clean, modern code using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and optionally React, Tailwind CSS, or Bootstrap.

Do you handle hosting and domains?+

Yes. We can deploy your site to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages free of charge, or to your own hosting provider.

What if I'm not happy with the result?+

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee within 7 days if you're not satisfied.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes! If you already own a domain, we'll connect it for you. If not, we can register one on your behalf.

Do you offer Arabic / RTL support?+

Yes. We build bilingual sites with full right-to-left support for Arabic, Hebrew, and other RTL languages.
