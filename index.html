04 — ABOUT

A small studio
with big standards.

NEXIFING is a digital studio founded by Eyad and Hazem — two people who got tired of watching good ideas fail behind bad execution.

Most agencies hand your project between designers, developers and hosting providers. When something breaks, nobody owns it.

We don't do that. Design, code, deploy, maintain — one team handles the entire stack. If something goes wrong, you talk to the people who built it. No middlemen, no "that's not our department."

We take a limited number of projects per quarter. Fewer clients means more time on each — and a finished product instead of a half-built mess.
