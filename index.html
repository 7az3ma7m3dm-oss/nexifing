FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Nexifing.

How fast will I get my website? +

Most sites are delivered within 24 hours of your request. Complex projects with custom integrations may take up to 3 days. You'll get a preview link as soon as it's ready.

Do I own the code? +

Yes, 100%. Every line of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React code that Nexifing generates for you is yours to keep, modify, and host wherever you want. No lock-in.

Can I request changes after delivery? +

Absolutely. Every plan includes unlimited revision requests for 7 days after delivery. Pro and Enterprise plans include ongoing support and priority revisions.

What tech stack do you use? +

We generate clean, modern code using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and optionally React, Tailwind CSS, or Bootstrap — depending on what your project needs. Everything is responsive and optimized.

Do you handle hosting and domains? +

Yes. We can deploy your site to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages free of charge, or to your own hosting provider. We also help you connect a custom domain if you have one.

What if I'm not happy with the result? +

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee within 7 days if you're not satisfied. We'll also work with you to fix any issues before issuing a refund — your success is our success.
