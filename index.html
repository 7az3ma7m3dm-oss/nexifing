FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you need to know about Nexifing.

How fast will I get my website?+

Most sites are delivered within 24 hours of your request. Complex projects with custom integrations may take up to 3 days. You'll get a preview link as soon as it's ready.

Do I own the code?+

Yes, 100%. Every line of HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React code that Nexifing generates for you is yours to keep, modify, and host wherever you want. No lock-in.

Can I request changes after delivery?+

Absolutely. Every plan includes unlimited revision requests for 7 days after delivery. Pro and Enterprise plans include ongoing support and priority revisions.

What tech stack do you use?+

We generate clean, modern code using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and optionally React, Tailwind CSS, or Bootstrap — depending on what your project needs.

Do you handle hosting and domains?+

Yes. We can deploy your site to Vercel, Netlify, or GitHub Pages free of charge, or to your own hosting provider. We also help you connect a custom domain.

What if I'm not happy with the result?+

We offer a 100% money-back guarantee within 7 days if you're not satisfied. We'll work with you to fix any issues before issuing a refund.

Can I use my own domain?+

Yes! If you already own a domain, we'll connect it for you. If not, we can register one on your behalf or recommend the best registrar for your country.

Do you offer Arabic / RTL support?+

Yes. We build bilingual sites with full right-to-left support for Arabic, Hebrew, and other RTL languages. The site will automatically adapt based on the language.

How do payments work?+

We accept cards, PayPal, and bank transfers. For Egyptian clients, we also accept local payment methods. You pay 50% upfront and the rest after you approve the preview.

Will my site work on mobile?+

Absolutely — every Nexifing site is mobile-first and tested on iPhone, Android, tablets, laptops, and ultrawide monitors. Your visitors will get a perfect experience on any device.

Can I add e-commerce to my site?+

Yes. We integrate Stripe, PayPal, and other payment providers. For Egyptian clients we also support local gateways like Fawry and Paymob.

Do you offer maintenance plans?+

Yes. Optional monthly maintenance plans start at $29/mo and include content updates, security monitoring, and priority support.

Can you redesign my existing site?+

Definitely. Send us the URL of your current site and we'll audit it, then either rebuild it from scratch or modernize specific sections — your choice.

What about analytics and tracking?+

We set up privacy-friendly analytics (Plausible, Umami, or Google Analytics 4) so you can see traffic, conversions, and user behavior without invasive tracking.

Do you sign NDAs?+

Yes. If your project is confidential, we're happy to sign a mutual NDA before we start. Just mention it in your request and we'll send one over.
