06 — PRINCIPLES

How we think

Six principles that shape every project we take on. These aren't marketing — they're filters for what we say yes to.

01

Ship working software

Nothing leaves our hands until it actually works. No "we'll fix that later", no placeholder buttons, no fake features.

02

Own the whole stack

Design, code, deploy, maintain. We don't hand off responsibility to other vendors — everything is on us.

03

Write for the next dev

Every line is documented, every decision is written down. If you leave us tomorrow, someone else can take over without a two-week handoff.

04

Say no when it matters

If something is a bad idea, we'll tell you. If a deadline is unrealistic, we'll say so. Better an honest no than a broken promise.

05

Security is not optional

Every server hardened, every API authenticated, every secret rotated. We treat your data like it's ours.

06

Stay small on purpose

We take a limited number of projects per quarter. Fewer clients means more time on each one — and a finished product instead of a half-built mess.

09 — FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything you'd want to know before starting a project with us.

How much does a project cost?

It depends on scope. A landing page starts around 3,000 EGP. A full web application with a backend and admin panel usually starts at 15,000 EGP. We'll give you a fixed quote after the first conversation — no hourly billing, no surprises.

How long does a project take?

Landing pages: 3–7 days. Full websites: 2–4 weeks. Complex apps: 4–8 weeks. We agree on a timeline up front and stick to it. If something changes, you hear it from us first.

Do you handle hosting and domains too?

Yes. We configure hosting, connect your domain, set up SSL, and manage deployment. We can host on GitHub Pages, Vercel, Netlify, or a VPS — whichever fits your project best. You own the account, we just help you set it up.

Do I own the code?

Always. Everything we build is yours — the source code, the deployment, the domain. We just help you get it live and keep it running. No lock-in, no proprietary platforms, no ransom for access.

What happens after the project is delivered?

Every project includes 30 days of free bug fixes. After that, we offer optional monthly retainers for updates, monitoring, and support. If you'd rather handle it yourself, we make sure the code is well documented so you can.

Do you work with clients outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with clients across the Middle East and worldwide. Communication is in English or Arabic, whichever you prefer. Payments accepted via InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer, or crypto.

What if I need changes during the project?

Minor changes are included. Major scope additions are quoted separately and agreed before we start them — so there are no surprise bills at the end.

Can you take over a project another developer started?

Yes — we do this often. Send us the codebase or a link to the repo and we'll audit it, tell you what's salvageable and what needs rebuilding, and give you a plan.

Do you offer refunds?

If we fail to deliver what we agreed on, you get a full refund of what's been paid. If you cancel mid-project, you pay only for the work completed up to that point.

What stack do you build with?

Mostly React/Next.js on the frontend, Node.js or Firebase on the backend, PostgreSQL or Firestore for data, and deploy to Vercel/Netlify or a Hetzner/DigitalOcean VPS. We'll recommend the right tools for your project, not the trendy ones.

Do you design too, or just build?

Both. We handle logo design, UI/UX, design systems, and brand guidelines — everything from the first sketch to the final pixel. If you already have a designer, we work with their files instead.

How do payments work?

Typically 50% up front, 50% on delivery. For larger projects we split into milestones. Payment methods: InstaPay, Vodafone Cash, bank transfer, or crypto for international clients.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes, on request. If your project involves sensitive information, we'll sign an NDA before any details are shared.

What if my project is unusual or complex?

We like unusual. Tell us what you're thinking — even if it's not in the list above. If we can build it, we'll say so. If we can't, we'll point you at someone who can.

How do I get started?

Send us a message using the contact form, or reach out on WhatsApp or Discord. Tell us what you're building, your timeline, and your budget range. We'll reply within 24 hours with next steps.

Have a project in mind?

Tell us what you're building. We'll reply within 24 hours with a plan and a price.
