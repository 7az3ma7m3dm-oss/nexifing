FAQ

Common questions

How much does a website cost?

Starter landing pages start at 3,000 EGP (~$60 USD). Full multi-page sites with admin panels start at 8,000 EGP. Full-stack builds start at 15,000 EGP. Every project gets a fixed quote before we start.

How long does it take?

Landing pages: 3–7 days. Multi-page sites: 1–2 weeks. Full applications: 2–4 weeks. Timelines are agreed upfront and rarely slip.

Do I own the code?

Yes. 100%. You get the full source code, database access and hosting credentials on final delivery.

What if something breaks after launch?

You get 30 days of free bug fixes after launch. After that, optional monthly maintenance plans start at 1,500 EGP.

Do you work outside Egypt?

Yes. We work with clients worldwide. Pricing is in EGP or USD depending on your location.

What payment methods?

Bank transfer, InstaPay, Vodafone Cash and PayPal for international clients. 50% upfront, 50% on delivery.
